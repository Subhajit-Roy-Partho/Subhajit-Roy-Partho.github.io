What I do

Four things I can bring to a team

Molecular simulation & force fields

Developer of oxDNA and oxView (CUDA and ARM support, merged upstream; Metal GPU backend on a separate branch). All-atom MD with AMBER, GROMACS, NAMD and OpenMM; enhanced sampling (umbrella sampling, REMD); MM/PBSA and MM/GBSA free-energy ranking.

Machine learning for molecules

fastDNA: an SE(3)-equivariant flow-matching model that generates 3D DNA/RNA conformations from sequence (in preparation), with a JAX path optimized for TPUs. PLAS-5k: a 5,000-complex protein–ligand affinity dataset (Scientific Data, 2022). oxView LLM: a fine-tuned Qwen model that drives simulation and analysis from natural language.

Scientific software & infrastructure

C++/CUDA, Python, TypeScript/WebGL. oxCloud (hosted simulation), nanobase (shared structures), SLURM/HPC campaigns with checkpoint-and-resume, and web tools used by experimental labs.

Closing the loop with experiments

Simulation-guided design that raised the growth temperature of a DNA origami crystal by 12 °C in the lab; all-atom modelling for DNA-templated PROTACs (J. Am. Chem. Soc., 2025); image analysis for DNA-PAINT and AFM.
